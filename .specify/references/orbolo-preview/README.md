# Orbolo preview site — image references

Full-size originals downloaded 2026-10-12 from https://orbolo-preview.vercel.app/assets,
the site the 2026-10-10 redesign was adapted from.

- `photos/` — every photo used on the preview site, plus `story-herd.webp` (its scroll-story image)
  and `og-cover.jpg` (its social preview image). Neither of those two is used on our site yet.
- `brand/` — wordmark in paper (`logo-paper.svg`) and brand green (`Orbolo_Logo_BrandGreen.svg`).
- `partners/` — WWF, MPCS and Fundación Paraguaya logos from the preview's partner strip. These are
  deliberately not on our site: listing partners is a content decision, not a design one.

Web-optimised copies (resized to max 2400px or 2000px, re-compressed) are what the site serves:
`public/images/site/`, `public/images/brand/`, and the three path-card photos in `public/images/paths/`
(investors = orbolo-gauchos, buyers = orbolo-herd, landowners = orbolo-palms).
